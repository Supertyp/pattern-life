Pattern languages were introduced by the architect Christopher Alexander in *A Pattern Language* (1977) to capture good solutions in building and town design. Software engineers later adopted the idea to share design knowledge.

Pattern Life takes the same approach to everyday life. Using a new kitchen gadget, planning a safe hike or finding a parking spot in a crowded city are all recurring problems with known good solutions — but that knowledge is usually scattered across manuals, forums and personal experience.

By writing these solutions down in a consistent structure, Pattern Life makes them easy to find, compare and improve.
