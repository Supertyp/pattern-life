framing: Stitch neat, lasting embroidery by hand, from the first hoop to a finished piece.

Hand embroidery looks fiddly, but most frustrations come from a few basics: the wrong needle or thread, slack fabric, tangled floss or a design traced with the wrong pen. The patterns in this area cover those foundations, how to plan and finish a piece, and a set of core stitches, each with the small details that make it look neat.
