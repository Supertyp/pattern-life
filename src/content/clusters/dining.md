---
title: Table and dining patterns
framing: Lay the table and enjoy a meal together, with good manners that make eating easier rather than stiffer.
order: 11
---

Table manners are mostly practical: cutlery laid in the order you'll use it, a glass where your hand expects it, food eaten in a way that keeps the tablecloth and your shirt clean. The patterns in this area cover setting a table for an everyday meal and eating dishes that can be tricky to eat neatly.
