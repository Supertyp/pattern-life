---
title: Jupyter notebook patterns for HASS
framing: Use Jupyter notebooks in humanities, arts and social sciences research so that your work can be rerun, shared and cited.
order: 12
---

Jupyter notebooks mix code, results and explanation in one document, which suits the exploratory, interpretive work of the humanities, arts and social sciences (HASS). The same freedom makes it easy to end up with notebooks that only run on one laptop, in one order, on one afternoon. The patterns in this area cover how to organise a notebook project, write notebooks that explain themselves, collect data from APIs responsibly, and share and archive the result so that others can rerun it and cite it.
