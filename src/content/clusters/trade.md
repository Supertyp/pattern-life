---
title: Trade agreement patterns
framing: Negotiate international trade agreements that meet their goals, survive domestic approval and actually get used.
order: 13
---

A trade agreement takes years to negotiate and decades to live with. Most of the problems that surface late (a chapter nobody can implement, an industry that was never consulted, a parliament that won't approve the result, or tariff cuts that exporters don't use) start with decisions made early. The patterns in this area follow an agreement from scoping and the negotiating mandate, through the rounds of negotiation, to signing, ratification and making it work in practice.
