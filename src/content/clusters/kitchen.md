title: Kitchen patterns
framing: Get reliable results from kitchen gadgets and techniques, without wasted food, broken equipment or burnt fingers.
order: 1
---

Modern kitchens are full of gadgets — pressure cookers, air fryers, stick blenders, sous-vide sticks — and each comes with a thick manual that few people read. The patterns in this area capture the handful of habits that make the difference between a gadget that gets used every week and one that collects dust.
